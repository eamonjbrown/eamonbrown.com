Work About

Brown Ensemble

Source
Bob Brown, 1450–1950 (1929)
Medium
Generative animation
Status
Ongoing

Brown Ensemble is a generative animation made from one book, Bob Brown’s 1450–1950.

Every word, phrase and drawing in the book was cut out of 600 dpi scans and traced as a filled vector outline. There are 1,658 of these, which I call atoms. Every mark on screen is from the book; nothing is set in a font or redrawn.

A small group of performers moves across the page. Each one holds an atom and morphs into another. What it picks next is steered by a similarity map of Bob’s marks, so a word tends to drift to the same word in another hand, to a rhyme or a shared stem, or to a drawing that looks like it. The performers move toward and away from the viewer, and when two meet, one takes the other’s form and the other leaves.

The full piece is a native app that reads a Muse S Athena headband: arousal, focus and breathing set the tempo, the size and weight of the marks, and how far each choice strays. The web version reads your cursor or finger instead. Movement makes the marks larger and brighter and the choices wider; keeping still keeps each performer close to related marks. Click or tap to call the nearest one forward.
