Brown Ensemble

Source
Bob Brown, 1450–1950 (1929)
Medium
Generative animation
Status
Ongoing

Brown Ensemble is a generative animation made from one book, Bob Brown’s 1450–1950.

Every word, phrase and drawing in the book was cut out of 600 dpi scans and traced as a filled vector outline. There are 1,658 of these, which I call atoms. Every mark on screen is from the book; nothing is set in a font or redrawn.

The web version joins the atoms back into the lines, stanzas and drawings-with-words they were cut from, using their positions on the page: about 350 passages. A small ensemble of performers moves across the page. Each holds one passage and morphs into another: often the one that follows it in the poem, otherwise a relative, chosen first from the same page of the book and then from a similarity map of Bob’s marks. Some are solid black ink and some are paler washes behind them.

Moving the cursor widens the choices. Click or tap to call the nearest figure forward.

The full piece is a native app that reads a Muse S Athena headband: arousal, focus and breathing set the tempo, the size and weight of the marks, and how far each choice strays.
